Spinning Mill Energy Consumption

Fiber to Yarn

Electricity and thermal energy used to convert staple fibers into wound yarn packages.

Blowroom, carding, drawing, roving, ring/rotor frames, winding
Heating, steaming and mill climate air conditioning
On-site rooftop solar or dedicated captive wind generation
Yarn conditioning humidification and fiber spray

Process Waste & Fiber Loss

Material Efficiency

Pneumatic droppings, card waste, comber noils, and roving waste generated during spinning.

Carded yarn: ~4-6%, Combed ring yarn: ~12-18%, Open-end rotor: ~3-5%

Yarn Dyeing Process

Package, cone, hank or continuous warp dyeing before fabric formation with dyes, electrolytes and auxiliaries.

Package dyeing typically 1:6 to 1:10; hank dyeing 1:15 to 1:20

Yarn Dyeing Energy Consumption

Circulation pumps, hydro-extractors, rapid dryers
Liquor heating, high-temp fixation, hot soaping
